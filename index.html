Phase 4 — Token economy
Tokenomics simulation & parameter review
Model the white paper parameters before any of them are built. The published figures currently allow $OZN to be minted far below intended cost: tipping earns 10 PRG per US$1, and 100 PRG staked wins 1 $OZN for 50 PRG, so self-dealing between accounts mints $OZN for the transaction fee alone. Needs sybil controls, or PRG per dollar decoupled from staking cost, before contracts are written.
L
  • Model PRG emissions ladder against projected activity
  • Model $OZN mint rate from Proof-of-Progress under realistic PRG supply
  • Close the self-dealing tipping loop (10 PRG per US$1 vs 50 PRG per $OZN)
  • Sybil-resistance requirements and cost-per-fake-account analysis
  • Activity buff caps and runaway-emission bounds
  • Inactivity decay and treasury recycling behaviour
  • Publish revised parameter table and reconcile against the white paper
LegalRegulatory & custody assessment
External counsel on $OZN and the public ICO across operating jurisdictions, covering MiCA, FCA registration and securities treatment. Determines custodial versus self-custody wallets. A gate, not a task: the answers change the architecture, and the exposure sits with the operating entity rather than any contractor.
L
  • External counsel engagement across operating jurisdictions
  • MiCA classification of $OZN and PRG
  • FCA registration requirements and financial-promotions scope
  • Securities treatment of the ICO and of founding-member PRG grants
  • Custodial vs self-custody determination (drives the wallet architecture)
  • KYC / AML obligations and provider selection
