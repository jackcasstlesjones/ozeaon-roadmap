The proposal

One release now · 12 days

The specification describes seven groups of notifications, delivered in four places: an unread count, a dropdown panel, a full notifications page, and a settings screen. It is more than one release. The table below splits it into four, and only the first one is what we are proposing to build now.

What we are building now

The 12-day first release, and nothing else. It delivers every notification the platform can genuinely send today, on the unread count, the dropdown panel and the notifications page.

Not in the 12 days

These are real, separately quoted pieces of work. Agreeing the 12 days does not buy them:

  • Accept and decline directly from a notification (quick actions)
  • The notification settings screen and per-alert controls
  • Anything to do with payouts, funding rounds, contributions or rewards
  • Grouping, automatic clearing, and instant live delivery

Included in the 12 days

Five groups

Everything here is driven by a part of the platform that is already live, so the notification is the only thing being built.

Group Area of the platform Covers
3A Community posts Tags, mentions, reposts, comments and reactions on posts (A1-A7)
3B Projects Team invitations and additions, comments, and linked projects (B1-B5)
3C Articles Co-authorship, organisation links and comments (C1-C4, C6)

Where we are starting from

Nothing in the product sends a notification today. There is an unused placeholder from the original build, but it cannot carry what the specification asks for - who did the thing, what it was about, which of your accounts it belongs to, or when you read it. So this is a build from scratch rather than an extension, which is what the first five days of the timeline reflect.

One thing already works in our favour: switching between your personal account and an organisation is live, and that is what lets a notification be addressed to an organisation rather than a person.

Not included in the 12 days

Five exclusions

The 12-day release is deliberately narrower than the specification. Everything listed below is outside it. None of it is built, none of it is priced into the 12 days, and each one needs its own go-ahead before it happens.

  1. Quick actions - not included. Invitations and join requests link through to the relevant screen. Accepting or declining from inside the notification itself is separate work.
  2. The settings screen - not included. The specification has every alert on by default, so launching with nothing to switch off is coherent - but there is no settings screen and no per-alert control in this release. One default still needs deciding, see below.
  3. Grouping - not included. "Five people liked your post" arrives as five separate notifications, not one. Noisier, but nothing is broken, and switching grouping on later needs no rework.
  4. Automatic clearing of old notifications - not included. The 30-day and one-year rules do not bite for 30 days and a year respectively. The groundwork goes in now and the clearing is added before the first ones age out.
  5. Instant live delivery - not included. The unread count refreshes on a short interval rather than the moment something happens. The specification does not ask for instant, and it costs noticeably more to run.

On top of these, the whole of payments, funding rounds and contributions is out - see the section below. Those groups cannot be built yet at any price.

Not included - payments & funding

Cannot be built yet

Just under half the list describes events the platform cannot yet produce, because the feature that would produce them has not been built. These are not cheaper to build later - they are not buildable now.

Group Waiting on
3D1-4 Payments. There is no payment provider connected yet, so there are no payouts or transactions to notify anyone about.
3F Funding rounds. Projects carry a funding target and a total raised, but named rounds - "Spring 2026" - do not exist as something the platform can reference, and nothing yet updates the amount raised.
3G Contributions, refunds and rewards. None of it is built.
C5 Article reviews. The feature does not exist yet.
D9 Role changes. Already flagged as blocked in the discovery document.

Any estimate for these groups covers the notifications work only. Connecting a payment provider, building funding rounds, and building contributions are separate projects with their own estimates - they are prerequisites, not line items here.

A few of these notifications are also time-based - "ends in three days" - which means the platform needs to be able to send something on a schedule rather than in response to an action. Nothing in the first release is time-based, which keeps that work out of it too.

Open questions

Five for design
  • The designs are marked in progress. All four screens are drawn and the settings screen is detailed down to the wording, but this estimate assumes no further structural change. If the layouts move, the front-end figure moves with them.
  • Every notification type needs its own icon. The specification says design assigns them. Until that list exists the notification row cannot be finished - and the same row is used in both the dropdown and the page.
  • How long the dropdown is, and how you see more. The specification says "between 10 and 25, design sets the exact number", and leaves open whether you scroll or press a button for more. Those are two different builds.
  • What mobile offers. The notifications page has seven actions on desktop; design chooses which of them mobile gets, and how marking something as read works on a touch screen.
  • "Mark all as read" currently means two things. In the dropdown it clears only what is on screen; on the page it clears everything. Both are reasonable - we just need confirmation that the difference is intended.

Decisions needed before we start

Seven items
  1. Confirm the scope is the 12-day release only, and that the five exclusions and the payments and funding groups are accepted as outside it.
  2. Confirm the designs are final, or accept the front-end estimate as provisional.
  3. Assign an icon to each notification type.
  4. Set the dropdown length, and whether seeing more is a scroll or a button.
  5. Choose which actions the notifications page offers on mobile.
  6. Decide the default for cross-account visibility - whether someone acting for an organisation sees that organisation's notifications alongside their own from day one. Settings are not in the first release, so whatever we pick is what everyone gets.
  7. Confirm the blocked groups are deferred, and that payments, funding rounds and contributions are estimated as projects in their own right.
